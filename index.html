Playback speed and pitch move together per note — that pitch/speed link is the classic YTPMV chipmunk sound, not a bug. Recording captures the canvas + audio live, so keep this tab in the foreground while it runs. Pitch detection is a rough guess from autocorrelation, not a tuner — always sanity-check the base note. Every clip is auto-leveled to a consistent peak on load, and a limiter sits on the master bus so stacked notes won't distort — the volume sliders and the "level auto-matched" note in a clip's status are stacked on top of that, not instead of it. Your edit decisions (trims, pitch, volumes, settings, and the MIDI itself) auto-save to this browser as you work — the clip files themselves are never copied, only remembered by name, so after a crash or closed tab you'll resume and relink your clips rather than losing everything. Clear the saved project if you'd rather start clean.
